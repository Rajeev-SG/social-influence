# Narration & storyboard

**Runtime:** 51.15 seconds; 30 fps, 1080 × 1920. **Voice:** Kokoro bf_emma, British English, locally synthesised. Sentence timing is measured from the generated audio, not estimated from word counts.

## Final narration

How does ChatGPT actually know what to say?

It doesn't look up a finished answer.

First, your message becomes tokens: small pieces of text.

Inside a neural network, attention connects each piece to relevant context.

Take: The cat sat on the...

Mat fits better than moon.

The model assigns probabilities to possible next tokens, then selects one.

Add it to the text. Run the network again.

That's a response, built one piece at a time.

But where did those abilities come from?

Training adjusted billions of internal settings, learning patterns from examples.

Answering uses those settings. It doesn't retrain the model with every reply.

From that simple loop emerge explanations, code, and ideas.

But a likely next word isn't a guarantee of truth.

Powerful prediction. Not perfect knowledge.

### 01 / 00.00–05.80s — Hook

- **0.30–3.22s:** How does ChatGPT actually know what to say?
- **3.45–5.50s:** It doesn't look up a finished answer.

### 02 / 05.80–09.68s — Tokens

The chat sentence separates into five individually animated dimensional tokens. Token IDs resolve, numerical vector bars form, then the pieces stream into the network.

- **5.80–9.45s:** First, your message becomes tokens: small pieces of text.

### 03 / 09.68–14.60s — Network

A mathematical 3D lattice slowly turns and expands. Pulses follow actual inter-layer edges; input token IDs travel through the volume. Attention is identified as information mixing.

- **9.68–14.37s:** Inside a neural network, attention connects each piece to relevant context.

### 04 / 14.60–18.39s — Attention

The cat sentence is suspended above a candidate token. Relevance is encoded in line thickness, opacity and energy moving along the curves. Mat resolves while moon recedes.

- **14.60–16.23s:** Take: The cat sat on the...
- **16.46–18.16s:** Mat fits better than moon.

### 05 / 18.39–23.06s — Prediction

Four volumetric probability towers grow from the same baseline: mat 68%, floor 21%, bed 8%, moon 3%. The selected tower is bracketed. Values are explicitly illustrative.

- **18.39–22.83s:** The model assigns probabilities to possible next tokens, then selects one.

### 06 / 23.06–28.62s — Generation

A context-reading loop surrounds the model. Mat and then punctuation physically travel from the model to the sentence; the return path shows that output becomes new input.

- **23.06–25.53s:** Add it to the text. Run the network again.
- **25.76–28.24s:** That's a response, built one piece at a time.

### 07 / 28.62–36.05s — Training

A question becomes a learning headline. Example streams feed a dimensional field of tuning controls. Settings change, converge, and settle in a final freeze sweep.

- **28.62–30.76s:** But where did those abilities come from?
- **30.99–35.82s:** Training adjusted billions of internal settings, learning patterns from examples.

### 08 / 36.05–40.93s — Inference

The same learned settings remain visually fixed as their field pulls back. Input and output signals pass around it on a new path: computation without training again.

- **36.05–40.63s:** Answering uses those settings. It doesn't retrain the model with every reply.

### 09 / 40.93–47.75s — Payoff

A single branching spine types examples of explanation, code and creative writing. The second spoken line replaces these with an explicitly false Moon statement and a fluency-versus-truth distinction.

- **40.93–44.52s:** From that simple loop emerge explanations, code, and ideas.
- **44.75–47.52s:** But a likely next word isn't a guarantee of truth.

### 10 / 47.75–51.15s — End

The camera pulls back on a unified mini-system: text pieces, contextual network, probability distribution, selected token, and the feedback path. The closing distinction remains dominant.

- **47.75–50.15s:** Powerful prediction. Not perfect knowledge.

## Art direction

A dark, editorial technical film rather than a simulated software demo. Information is organised through scale, depth, light and motion. Palette: #080e18, #f1f0e8, #7aefde, #528dff; violet for secondary channels and amber only for uncertainty. Generous mobile-safe margins. Sentence captions are a deliberately quiet supporting layer. No stock assets. Masked scene handoffs retain continuity without double-exposing headlines.
