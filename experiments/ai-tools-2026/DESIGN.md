# AI Tools 2026 — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Cinematic technical documentary meets premium product keynote: deep blue-black space,
precise white typography, controlled cyan instrumentation and warm amber signals. The
camera and typography move with weight and purpose. Every scene behaves like a measured
data instrument rather than a presentation slide.

## Colors

- `#071018` — canvas, tinted near-black
- `#EAF6FF` — primary type
- `#68E0FF` — intelligence and active data
- `#FFB45C` — attention, rank and conclusion
- `#253746` — structural rules and inactive data

## Typography

- Display: `Manrope`, weights 700–800.
- Data and labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, weights 400–600.

## Motion Rules

- Build every hero layout in its final readable state before adding motion.
- Use short, asymmetric entrances and slow ambient camera drift.
- Chart values must move together with narration, never as decoration.
- Preserve visual continuity through luminous sweeps, depth planes and color remapping.
- Banned: generic card grids, stock imagery, fake product screenshots, repetitive zooms,
  gradient text and decorative effects that do not explain the story.
