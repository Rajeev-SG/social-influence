# Usage Ranking and Product Claims

## Ranking Method

The primary on-screen ranking uses one consistent metric: **worldwide generative-AI
website-visit share across seven assistant websites, September 2026 (Similarweb)**.
This is website traffic, not total registered users, monthly active users, API traffic,
app usage, or embedded usage. Meta AI is discussed separately because its primary usage
is embedded inside WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook and is not part of that seven-site
comparison.

## Product Differentiators

- **ChatGPT:** broad multi-modal assistant, general-purpose writing and reasoning,
  creation, files, voice and agentic workflows.
- **Gemini:** Google ecosystem integration, multimodal work, long context and
  distribution through Search, Android and Workspace.
- **Claude:** careful long-form writing and analysis, coding and agent-oriented
  development workflows.
- **DeepSeek:** cost-efficient model access and open-model influence; weaker as an
  all-purpose consumer product than the distribution leaders.
- **Copilot:** contextual assistance inside Microsoft 365, Windows and enterprise data
  permissions; standalone web visits understate that embedded usage.
- **Perplexity:** source-forward, search-shaped research with visible citations.
- **Grok:** live, conversational and closely connected to X's real-time public stream.
- **Meta AI:** embedded social assistant distributed through Meta's messaging and social
  apps; scale is not comparable to a standalone website traffic ranking.

Capability descriptions are concise editorial characterisations of the products' main
market positions, not benchmark claims. Product names and colors are used nominatively
for comparison; no proprietary interface screenshots are presented as genuine.

Company-reported weekly/monthly totals, Sensor Tower audience estimates, enterprise
spend and OpenRouter request volume answer different questions and are deliberately not
mixed into the web-traffic bars. In particular, Microsoft Copilot's standalone website
traffic understates embedded Microsoft 365/Windows usage, while Meta AI's reach is
distributed through WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
