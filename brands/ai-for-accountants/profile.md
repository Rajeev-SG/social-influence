# AIForAccountants — creation profile

## Audience
Owners, partners, managers and technically curious senior staff in small accounting practices, initially UK-heavy. Keep workflows jurisdiction-neutral when possible.

## Positioning
**Practical AI workflows for accounting firms — automate the admin, keep the judgement.**

## Content promise
Every video answers:
1. What task?
2. Exactly how?
3. What can go wrong?
4. What must the human check?

## Durable pillars
- client communications
- Excel / spreadsheet analysis
- document and meeting workflows
- practice operations / SOPs
- tool comparisons
- privacy / accuracy controls

## Format mix
- 40% screen-recorded workflow
- 20% manual vs AI-assisted process
- 15% prompt/template transformation
- 15% tool comparison/test
- 10% failure/risk/control example

## Hook patterns
- "Accountants: stop using ChatGPT for this task like this."
- "This 25-minute client-admin job becomes a five-minute review workflow."
- "Before you paste client data into an AI tool, check these three settings."
- "AI should draft this. It should not approve it."
- "Here's one accounting workflow I would automate — and one I wouldn't."
- "Your firm's AI policy can start with this one-page rule."
- "Watch me turn this ugly spreadsheet into a review-ready summary."
- "Most AI prompts for accountants are useless because they leave out these four fields."
- "Can AI reconcile this correctly? Let's test it instead of trusting the demo."
- "If your team uses AI already but nobody can explain the controls, start here."

## Tone
Accountant-to-accountant: calm, concrete, operational and slightly sceptical of vendor hype. Never generic "10 insane AI hacks".

## Pacing
- Show the task/problem immediately.
- Crop tightly to one operation at a time.
- Fast cursor/zoom/highlight guidance.
- Keep demonstrations short enough to show one useful workflow, not a complete training course.
- Show the human review step before the payoff/CTA.

## Visual guidance
- Competent operator's desktop, not futuristic AI advertising.
- Use synthetic/public accounting datasets only.
- Real screen recordings: spreadsheets, PDFs, email drafts, workflow diagrams, AI interfaces.
- Persistent status strip: **INPUT → AI STEP → HUMAN CHECK**.
- Reusable callouts: **SAFE TO AUTOMATE**, **REVIEW REQUIRED**, **DO NOT UPLOAD CLIENT DATA**.
- No avatar required; neutral voiceover + cursor movement is preferred.
- Never show genuine client data.

## CTA options
- "Save this workflow."
- "Comment the accounting task you still do manually."
- "Get the review checklist."
- Later: "Workflow library in bio."

## Evidence / QA
- Vendor capability claims come from current vendor documentation plus independent testing where practical.
- Standards/tax assertions come from the relevant official regulator/professional body.
- UK accounting-standard claims should anchor to FRC material.
- Do not invent productivity savings; measure them if used.
- Any workflow output that could affect a filing, accounting judgement or client advice must show the human review/control point.

## Forbidden / avoid
- autonomous tax/accounting advice presented as authoritative
- fabricated standards/citations
- telling users to submit filings without review
- genuine identifiable/confidential client data
- claims that software is compliant merely because it uses encryption
- guaranteed productivity percentages
- fake AI accountant/CPA persona

## Preferred Content Machine shape
- Archetypes: `howto`, `versus`, `product-demo`
- Visual lane: screen-recording + motion-card overlays.
- Publish target: TikTok, Reels, Shorts.
